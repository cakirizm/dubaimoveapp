# Dubai Move — Privacy & Data-Minimization Rules

Dubai Move is practical relocation assistance, not a legal-advice or government-impersonation product.

## Core rules

- Private by default.
- Collect data only when a specific move task needs it.
- Never expose tenancy contracts, identity documents, home photos, phone numbers or private chats to unrelated providers.
- Provider access must be scoped to the selected request/booking.
- Regulated assistance requires service-specific provider verification.
- Official/government fees and provider fees must remain separate.
- AI findings are suggestions only until the user confirms them.
- AI damage detection must never label liability or legal responsibility.
- AI video inventory output may be shared only after user review/confirmation.
- Status-sharing links expose operational status only by default; documents, photos, chats and phone numbers are excluded.
- Sharing links must support expiry and revocation.
- Landlord handover reports include only explicitly relevant handover evidence.
- Unrelated IDs, private chats and payment-card information must stay out of landlord PDFs.
- Exact GPS is optional unless a user explicitly enables a location-dependent feature. Address/building selection should also work through search/manual selection.
- Building access details such as vehicle/crew information are collected only when the building requires them.
- Transactional notification consent and marketing consent must remain separate.

## AI boundary

Dubai Move may:
- summarize user-provided information,
- compare before/after evidence,
- identify possible differences,
- extract structured fields from documents,
- estimate inventory,
- explain operational next steps,
- organize evidence and generate reports.

Dubai Move must not:
- provide legal advice,
- determine legal liability,
- claim an AI observation is conclusive damage,
- claim a government action was completed without an authorized integration/confirmed external state,
- silently share private user data.

## Provider boundary

For a marketplace request, providers receive the minimum scope needed to quote/perform that job. A moving provider does not automatically receive a tenancy contract, Emirates ID, DEWA bill or landlord communication. Regulated-service assistance must be gated by service-specific verification and expiry/availability checks.
